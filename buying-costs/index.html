Dubai Off-Plan Buying Costs 2026: DLD 4% Fee, Oqood Fee and Cash Needed on Day One

How much cash do you need to buy an off-plan property in Dubai? Three amounts are due at the start: the booking payment to the developer, the Dubai Land Department (DLD) transfer fee of 4% of the price, and the Oqood registration fee for off-plan sales.

What projects actually ask for

Across 259 off-plan projects with recorded 2026 sales, the median booking is 10% of the price. The most common booking levels are 10% (77 projects), 20% (67 projects), 15% (5 projects). The median cash needed on day one, using the median price paid in each project, is AED 280,985, against a median price of AED 1,307,099.

Agency commission, mortgage fees and service charges are extra and are not included here. The project-level figures are a free download on the Dubai property open data page, and individual recorded sales can be checked with the DLD transactions search.

How these figures are calculated

Each figure is the median of individual sales recorded by the Dubai Land Department (DLD) in 2026, up to 9 November 2026. These are prices buyers paid, not asking prices. An area needs at least 20 recorded sales before a median is published. See how we read the DLD register.

Source: Dubai Land Department open data, compiled by Certified Poor, which is independent and sells no property. Figures change as new sales are recorded and are provided as-is.
