Before you buy on price alone

A low price can reflect distance, smaller units or a project that is years from completion. Check how far a building has progressed with the Dubai project status tracker, and compare individual sales with the DLD transactions search. Every area and home type is in the free download on the Dubai property open data page.

How these figures are calculated

Each figure is the median of individual sales recorded by the Dubai Land Department (DLD) in 2026, up to 9 November 2026. These are prices buyers paid, not asking prices. An area needs at least 20 recorded sales before a median is published. See how we read the DLD register.

Source: Dubai Land Department open data, compiled by Certified Poor, which is independent and sells no property. Figures change as new sales are recorded and are provided as-is.
