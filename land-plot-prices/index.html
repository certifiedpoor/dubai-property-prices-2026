Across these 28 areas, 4,978 plots were sold. The lowest average is Madinat Hind 4 at AED 1,877,667 per plot and the highest is Palm Jabal Ali at AED 32,280,314.

Reading these figures

These are averages, not medians, and plot sizes differ a lot between areas, so use them as a guide to scale and not as a price for a specific plot. Many of these sales are plots inside villa communities. To look up recorded sales in one area, use the DLD transactions search. The underlying figures are a free download on the Dubai property open data page.

On this page the figures are averages of recorded plot sales, not medians. Counts cover plot sales recorded against a registered project, so an area's full total can be higher.

How these figures are calculated

Each figure is the median of individual sales recorded by the Dubai Land Department (DLD) in 2026, up to 9 November 2026. These are prices buyers paid, not asking prices. An area needs at least 20 recorded sales before a median is published. See how we read the DLD register.

Source: Dubai Land Department open data, compiled by Certified Poor, which is independent and sells no property. Figures change as new sales are recorded and are provided as-is.
